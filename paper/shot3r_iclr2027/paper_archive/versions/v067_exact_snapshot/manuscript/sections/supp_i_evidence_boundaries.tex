\section{Evidence Boundaries}

The supplementary material reports only studies with retained result records.
The following evidence is intentionally outside the present claim:
independently trained token-removal variants, the corresponding EgoBody direct
association audit, a second reconstruction backbone, natural edited-video
evaluation without geometric ground truth, a blind user study, and a complete
provenance reconstruction of the inherited \humanthree{} base model's
pretraining corpus. Same-checkpoint inference masks are retained only as
sensitivity diagnostics and are not substitutes for retraining.
These omissions delimit the strength of the reported causal camera--human
bridging evidence; they do not alter a recorded numerical result or justify a
proxy comparison.

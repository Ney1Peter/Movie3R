\section*{AI Use Statement}

Language-model assistance was used for planning, prose editing, analysis
support, code scaffolding, and editable vector layout and annotation. It did
not synthesize evaluation inputs, reconstruction panels, ground truth, or
numerical measurements. The authors inspected the underlying records and are
responsible for every protocol, result, figure, citation, and claim.

\section*{Ethics Statement}

The human-capture datasets are public or research-licensed and are used under
their providers' consent, access, and research-use conditions. We do not infer
participant identity, link participants across datasets, or use the material
for surveillance. Restricted imagery, annotations, and checkpoints remain in
access-controlled storage and are not redistributed when prohibited by their
licences.

\section*{Reproducibility Statement}

We retain fixed evaluation definitions, evaluator code, per-case records,
method-to-code mappings, streaming-input checks, a controlled state-isolation
experiment with identical weights, an alignment--state-propagation control,
viewpoint-clustered statistics, an association-cue ablation, and
repeated-transition controls. Tables are
generated from machine-readable summaries with source hashes. End-to-end
reproduction additionally requires the licensed data and checkpoints; release
materials will include code, protocol manifests, aggregate measurements, and
non-identifying case identifiers to the extent permitted by those licences.

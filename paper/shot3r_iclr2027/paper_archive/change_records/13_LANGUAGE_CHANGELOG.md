# v029 to v030 Language Changelog

- Replaced anthropomorphic state-ownership language with the operational rule
  that the clean-reset state is the only recurrent state propagated.
- Replaced broad editing language with the defined same-scene, synchronized or
  temporally adjacent viewpoint-cut setting.
- Replaced claims that gains are uniformly largest at wide views with the
  measured dataset-equal extreme-minus-non-extreme interaction.
- Replaced component-necessity wording with a complete-pathway claim; the
  available token masks are explicitly same-checkpoint sensitivity tests.
- Replaced generic camera ATE with ATE-Sim3 or ATE-SE3 according to the
  alignment group.
- Replaced single association percentages with conditional pair accuracy and
  full-continuation recovery denominators.
- Replaced preregistration wording with `pre-specified` where no public,
  time-stamped registration exists.
- Added direct language for retained adverse results: Harmony4D W-MPJPE,
  AIST++ anchored-root metrics, multi-cut camera drift, and MVHuman early
  detector triggers.
- Removed engineering revision names from rendered prose and method labels.

\section{Component Scope and Additional Evidence}
\label{app:components}

Table~\ref{tab:mechanism-evidence} is the matched-input mechanism study used in the
main paper. It separates the no-event reference, annotated-boundary state
reinitialization and camera-alignment controls, and the complete online system. The
counterfactual rows answer whether history-conditioned alignment can restore a supplied
transition; they are neither deployable baselines nor a one-variable additive
chain. Association is evaluated separately with explicit conditional and
continuation denominators.

The learned alignment pathway contains semantic, pose-alignment, and temporal
continuity tokens, but the retained component masks share one jointly trained
checkpoint. Such masks change the inference distribution and are not
equivalent to independently trained architectural variants. We therefore use
the system-level controls and direct association audits as the public
cross-dataset mechanism evidence. The same-checkpoint masking results are
sensitivity diagnostics only; they do not establish the necessity of an
individual token or residual head.

The remaining component evidence is reported in the dataset-specific sections:
the EgoHumans and Harmony4D association audits test prediction-only
correspondence at the first post-cut frame; the train-only blend grid tests the fixed
shared-translation coefficient without reselection; and the AIST++ repeated
cut studies test causal-event composition. Each study is kept separate because
it answers a different question and uses its own pre-specified evaluation set.

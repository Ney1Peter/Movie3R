\section*{AI Use Statement}

Language-model assistance was used to search and summarize relevant
literature; plan and edit the manuscript structure and prose; translate
between Chinese and English; discuss experimental design and interpret
diagnostic results; scaffold and review analysis and document-tooling code;
and prepare editable vector layouts and annotations. The authors independently
verified cited sources, code, protocols, numerical results, figures, and
claims against the underlying records. Generative AI was not used to produce
ground truth, evaluation inputs, or reconstruction results. The authors take
responsibility for the final content of this work.

\section*{Ethics Statement}

The human-capture datasets are public or research-licensed and are used under
their providers' consent, access, and research-use conditions. We do not infer
participant identity, link participants across datasets, or use the material
for surveillance. Restricted imagery, annotations, and checkpoints remain in
access-controlled storage and are not redistributed when prohibited by their
licences.

\section*{Reproducibility Statement}

Appendix~\ref{app:protocol} specifies the datasets, inputs, metrics, and
comparison protocol; Appendix~\ref{app:implementation} reports training and
implementation details; and Appendices~\ref{app:method-details} and
\ref{app:full-results} provide the method details, controlled analyses, and
complete results. Tables are generated from machine-readable summaries with
source hashes. End-to-end reproduction additionally requires the licensed data
and checkpoints. Release materials will include code, protocol manifests,
aggregate measurements, and non-identifying case identifiers to the extent
permitted by those licences.

The supplement is organized into three parts. Part I defines the evaluation
contract and documents training, inference, detector, runtime, and coordinate
conventions. Part II provides the complete comparisons and controlled evidence
under that contract. Part III states the supported scope and limitations.
Page numbers link to the corresponding sections.

\textbf{Highlights and suggested reading.}
For a compact path through the principal evidence, we recommend:

\vspace{4pt}
\begin{minipage}[t]{0.48\linewidth}
\textbf{Method and reproducibility.}
Section~\ref{app:protocol} fixes datasets, metrics, and comparison scope;
Sections~\ref{app:implementation}--\ref{app:method-details} document trainable
parameters, detector, runtime, coordinate transforms, and repeated inference.

\medskip
\textbf{State-transition evidence.}
Section~\ref{app:egohumans-state-isolation} gives the same-checkpoint
carry-over control; Sections~\ref{app:egobody-details}--\ref{app:harmony-details}
provide the corresponding reconstruction and association analyses.
\end{minipage}\hfill
\begin{minipage}[t]{0.48\linewidth}
\textbf{Viewpoint and repeated-transition evidence.}
Section~\ref{app:cross-dataset-viewpoint} analyzes viewpoint change, and
Section~\ref{app:multicut} verifies that the transition operation can be
reapplied on three-shot streams.

\medskip
\textbf{Visual and scope evidence.}
Section~\ref{app:qualitative-full} retains the full seven-method qualitative
comparison, while Section~\ref{app:limitations} states the supported scope and
remaining limitations.
\end{minipage}

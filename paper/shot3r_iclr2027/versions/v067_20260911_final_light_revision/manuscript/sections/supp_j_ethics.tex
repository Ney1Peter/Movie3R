\section{Data Governance, Privacy, and Assistance Disclosure}

The training and evaluation sources are public or research-licensed human
capture datasets. Some contain identifiable participants. We rely on the
consent, access, and research-use conditions established by the dataset
providers; we do not infer participant identity, link a participant across
datasets, or use the material for surveillance. Licensed RGB, calibration,
and body annotations remain in access-controlled local storage. A release
would contain code, protocol definitions, aggregate measurements, and
non-identifying identifiers, but not redistribute restricted source imagery or
checkpoints whose licences prohibit redistribution.

The reported experiments use real source frames, model predictions, camera
estimates, and evaluator outputs. Language-model assistance was used for
planning, prose editing, analysis support, code scaffolding, and editable
vector layout and annotations. It did not synthesize input observations,
reconstruction panels, ground truth, or numerical measurements. The authors
inspected the underlying records and remain responsible for every protocol,
figure, citation, and claim.

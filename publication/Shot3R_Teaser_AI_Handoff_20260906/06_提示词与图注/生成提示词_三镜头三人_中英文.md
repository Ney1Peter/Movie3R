Use case: scientific-educational.
Asset type: a polished conceptual teaser illustration for an ICLR paper on streaming multi-person 4D reconstruction.

Primary request: show exactly THREE distinct people, THREE consecutive shots with TWO abrupt viewpoint changes, and ONE coherent shared 3D world. This is a conceptual illustration, not a visualization of measured experimental outputs.

Composition: a wide, white-background scientific figure, approximately 2.5:1. The upper two-thirds is one continuous elevated three-quarter view of a spacious indoor room. Keep one connected floor, recognizable walls and windows, and a few understated pieces of furniture. Do not split the upper scene into three independent rooms. The lower third is a slim chronological input strip, grouped into three clearly separated shots, with two small frame thumbnails per shot. These depict successive times in a monocular stream, not simultaneous multi-camera observations.

People: three distinct human identities with consistent coral, turquoise and lavender colors. Show natural full-body interaction and movement. In the shared 3D scene, use smoothly shaded human meshes with subtle surface detail. Add a small number of lighter historical poses to suggest temporal progression; these are earlier instances of the same three people, not additional identities. Keep current poses visually dominant, with clear silhouettes and plausible anatomy.

Cameras and time: show small, precise wireframe camera frusta at positions associated with the three shots. Within each shot, a short sequence of frusta suggests sequential updates. At the two shot transitions, the camera position and viewing direction change substantially, while the room and identity colors remain consistent. Do not depict these as a synchronized multi-camera rig. Let the input strip read from left to right, with visibly different viewing directions in the three groups.

Style: refined technical 3D illustration inspired by modern computer-vision paper teasers. Muted scene texture and selectively visible point-cloud structure; crisp, saturated human colors; soft shadows; convincing depth; clean margins; strong visual hierarchy. Prioritize the people, spatial continuity, and viewpoint changes. Avoid excessive wireframe clutter, oversized cameras, decorative neon, or a gaming-poster aesthetic.

Text: no title, labels, numbers, metrics, dataset names, logos, or watermark. Leave small clean spaces for editable typography to be added later. Do not copy text from the references.

Constraints: exactly three distinct identities throughout; three temporally ordered shots; two shot changes; one shared space; no additional people; no invented performance claims. The final figure will be explicitly captioned as a conceptual illustration.

## 对应中文提示词

```text
用途：科学概念示意图。
图像类型：用于 ICLR 流式多人体四维重建论文的精致 teaser 概念插图。

核心要求：恰好三个不同人物、按时间连续排列的三个镜头、两次明显视角跳变，以及一个连贯统一的三维世界。该图是概念示意，不是已测实验输出的可视化。

构图：白底横向科学插图，宽高比约 2.5:1。上方约三分之二是一整个宽敞室内空间的斜上方三维全景，有连贯的地面、可辨认的墙和窗，以及少量不抢眼的家具。不能将上方画成三个独立房间。下方约三分之一是一条细长的输入时间带，分为三个清楚的镜头组，每组两张小帧图。小图代表单目视频流中先后发生的时刻，不是同一时刻的多相机同步观测。

人物：三个身份始终分别使用珊瑚橙、青绿和淡紫配色。表现自然的全身互动与移动。上方使用平滑着色、带少量表面细节的人体网格。以少量更浅的历史姿态表现时间推进；这些是同三个人在较早时刻的姿态，不是新增身份。当前姿态最醒目，人体轮廓清楚、比例合理。

相机与时间：在与三个镜头对应的位置画小而精致的线框相机视锥。每个镜头内用一小段视锥序列表现逐帧更新。在两次镜头切换处，相机位置和观察方向明显变化，但室内世界与人物身份颜色保持一致。不要画成多相机同步采集装置。下方输入从左向右推进，三个镜头组的观看方向有明显差异。

风格：参考近期计算机视觉论文 teaser 的精致技术三维插画。场景纹理柔和，可局部表现点云结构；人体配色清晰醒目；柔和阴影、有空间纵深、边缘留白干净、主次明确。重点突出人物、空间接续和视角跳变。避免杂乱线框、过大的相机、装饰性霓虹和游戏海报风。

文字：不生成标题、标签、数字、指标、数据集名、标识或水印。留出少量干净区域，方便后续添加可编辑文字。不要复制参考图中的文字。

约束：始终只有三个不同身份；三个按时间排列的镜头；两次切镜；统一空间；不出现额外人物；不编造性能指标。最终图注将明确说明这是概念示意。
```
